Global Recognition and Achievements

One brush. One world. One humanity.

People from around the world became part of a single movement, where each person passed the brush as a symbol of peace, connecting continents, cultures, and destinies.

Guinness World Record Holder

World Painting set a world record for the largest number of people in an online video chain passing a paintbrush from one to another.

01 World Painting transcended the boundaries of our planet and became a symbol of humanity not only on Earth, but in space. A digital copy of the World Painting was sent to the International Space Station. 02 There, in orbit around the Earth, it was printed on a mission timeline - a working document used by astronauts, becoming part of everyday life aboard the station. 03 The painting was printed by Russian cosmonaut Anton Shkaplerov, after which a message to all humanity was sent from orbit. In that moment, the World Painting gained a new meaning. It moved beyond Earth and became a symbol of the unity of humanity in the universe.

Beyond Earth

At the Top of the World

The World Painting flag, created on canvas, was raised to the highest point on Earth - the summit of Mount Everest.

This ascent was accomplished by Maksut Zhumayev - captain of the Kazakhstan national mountaineering team, and a climber who has conquered all 14 eight-thousanders of the world.

The Coin of Peace

The National Bank of the Republic of Kazakhstan issued a commemorative coin dedicated to the World Painting project.

At its center is the dove of peace - an image imbued with profound meaning and history.

It was created by artist Karipbek Kuyukov - a man who has become a symbol of resilience and humanism. Born without arms near the Semipalatinsk nuclear test site, he devoted his life to art and to the pursuit of peace, becoming a prominent voice of the anti-nuclear movement «ATOM».

The dove was created by the artist without the use of hands - as a deeply personal message to humanity about the value of life, peace, and a future free from nuclear threat.

The olive branch in the dove's beak was added by Dauren Kasteev - grandson of the renowned Kazakh artist Abylkhan Kasteev, symbolically uniting generations in a single artistic gesture.

This coin is more than a collector's item. It is a bearer of profound meaning and humanistic values.

For numismatists and collectors around the world, it stands as a unique artifact a symbol of peace, captured in metal.

A symbol of peace that will endure in history.

The Stamp of Peace

The World Painting postage stamp, issued by Kazpost, has become another symbol of the project. At its center is the dove of peace - a universal sign of humanity, hope, and unity.

A postage stamp is a language through which nations speak to one another, carrying thoughts and messages across borders.

The World Painting stamp conveys a simple idea: that only messages of peace, kindness, and understanding should travel between countries. Peace, passed from country to country.

Format: 40 x 28 mm

The World Painting Peace Belt

World Painting joined forces with the World Boxing Council WBC - one of the most influential sports organizations in the world.

This collaboration became a space where the power of sport and the language of art came together in a single message - a message of peace, humanity, and unity.

As part of the anniversary event «100 Knockout Night» at the legendary MGM Grand Arena in Las Vegas, 100 exclusive medals were created - symbols not only of sporting achievement, but of humanity's global aspiration for peace.

These medals were awarded to outstanding athletes and renowned boxers as a recognition of strength directed toward creation.

The idea continued with the World Painting Peace Belt - a special belt of peace created in collaboration with WBC.

It was presented to the legendary Wladimir Klitschko by Lennox Lewis - as a symbol of strength, responsibility, and peace.

The belt was presented to Terence Crawford, symbolically bringing sport and art together into a shared space of values.
